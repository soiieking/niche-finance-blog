StepGrab 1.4 is out, and you can finally edit arrows. Yes, arrows. Remember that thread on r/sideproject where at least eleven people said something along the lines of: "This tool is cool, but fixed arrows ruin it for me"? They listened. StepGrab's dev team (probably one person — let’s be real) rolled up their sleeves and made it happen.
Here’s how to use editable arrows in 1.4, what else has changed, and why it might just bump this app into your daily workflow. Or not. Your call.
## Quick Recap: What Is StepGrab?
In case you’re scratching your head, StepGrab is a lightweight tool for annotating step-by-step processes. You upload a screenshot or grab a window, then slap on numbers, labels, and arrows to guide your user/client/future self. The app’s big selling point? It’s blazing fast compared to Canva or Snagit. 
But until now, you were stuck with rigid arrows. Want to curve an arrow around an annoying element in your UI? Nope. Don’t like where the arrow’s tail starts? Tough luck.
## Why Editable Arrows Matter
Maybe you’re thinking, “It’s just arrows. Who cares?” Trust me, you care. 
Take this comment from u/makerperson22 in the v1.3 launch thread: “I use StepGrab for writing help docs, but the arrows are killing me. They’re stuck in weird places when I resize, and I can’t adjust the curvature. It’s unusable for polished work.” 
It wasn’t just them. Go scroll that thread — it’s wall-to-wall “arrows suck” replies. If you're documenting anything more complex than “Click here to log in,” bad arrows destroy clarity. And clarity’s, like, the whole point of StepGrab.
StepGrab 1.4 fixes that. Let’s dive into how.
## How to Use Editable Arrows in StepGrab 1.4
### Step 1: Upgrade to v1.4
First, make sure you’re on the latest version. If you’re using macOS or Windows:
1. **Download from the official site**: [stepgrab.com](https://stepgrab.com).
2. Overwrite your old install with the new one. (Settings are preserved.)
3. No Linux version yet — sorry. I’ll keep shouting at the dev(s) on your behalf.
Verify the version by opening the app and going to **Help > About**. It should say `v1.4.0 (Build 1042)` or higher.
### Step 2: Add a Screenshot or Annotation
- Open your screenshot.
- Click the **Arrow** tool in the sidebar as usual.
- Add an arrow anywhere. The UI looks the same, at first.
### Step 3: Edit That Arrow
Here’s where the magic kicks in:
1. Select the arrow you just placed.
2. Grab the **control points** (those tiny, draggable handles you’ll see now) to adjust curvature.
3. Drag the start or end-point anywhere you want. Absolute freedom.
Want to keep things simple? Just move the whole arrow like before.
### Step 4: Save or Export
Once your arrows look good enough for your boss/Reddit audience/self, save the project or export it as a PNG or SVG, like you always do.
### Pro Tip: Learn the Shortcuts
You can fine-tune faster with these new shortcuts:
- **Shift + Click** on an arrow: Snap the arrow’s handles to grid points.
- **Cmd/Ctrl + Click**: Reset curvature to "straight."
The dev also added a toggle for “Arrow snapping” in **Preferences**, which locks their angles to 15° increments. It’s a godsend for perfectionists.
## What Else Is New in v1.4?
While arrows stole the show, two low-key features are worth mentioning:
1. **Better SVG Export**: It now includes all your annotations as separate layers, instead of welding them into a nightmare blob. Perfect for Illustrator finaglers.
2. **Improved Performance on Large Screenshots**: A 6MB monster PNG no longer crashes the app. Tested this on a Dell XPS 15 (32GB RAM) and rendered one in ~1.8 seconds. Way faster than v1.3.
