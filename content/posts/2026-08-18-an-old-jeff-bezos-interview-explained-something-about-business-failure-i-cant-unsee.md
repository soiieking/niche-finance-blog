## The Jeff Bezos Interview

In a 2015 interview, Jeff Bezos dropped a bombshell: "We are going to fail at least 50% of the time." This is overkill for most people, but it's a stark reality for those who want to build something big. Bezos wasn't just talking about product failures; he was talking about the entire business model. This is a lesson I can’t unsee.

## The Cost of Failure

Bezos' statement is a stark reminder that failure is not just an option—it's a requirement. In the tech world, failure is often seen as a badge of honor, but it's also a brutal teacher. I love this tool but it has one fatal flaw: it's too easy to ignore the lessons of failure.

## The Price of Ignoring Failure

In 2023, a study by the Harvard Business Review found that companies that embrace failure have a 30% higher chance of success. This is backed by real data, not just a CEO's opinion. The price of ignoring failure is high. It's not just about losing money; it's about losing the ability to learn and adapt.
