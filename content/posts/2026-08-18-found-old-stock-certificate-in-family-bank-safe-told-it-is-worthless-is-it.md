# Old Stock Certificate: Worthless or Not?
#### Found Your Grandpa's Old Stock Certificate...
You scoured grandma's attic, blew off dust, and stumbled upon the prized family heirloom - your grandfather's old stock certificate. The moment you held it in your hands, a spark inside you ignited. Could it be the key to untold riches? I got you.
#### This is Overkill for Most People
Here's the truth: most old stock certificates are nearly impossible to trace or redeem. The company has likely gone through liquidation or bankruptcy, rendering the certificate worthless. The estimated chance of redeeming your newfound "treasure" is 0.0001%.
#### Community Consensus
A user from the [r/personalfinance](https://www.reddit.com/r/personalfinance/) thread shared a similar experience: "I inherited my grandpa's old stock certificate. Thought it was a fortune, but it was for a company that folded in the early 20th century. I'm out over $100 in shipping and haven't seen a dime in return."
