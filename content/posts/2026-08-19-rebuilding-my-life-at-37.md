## The Big Reset
I hit 37 and realized my life was a mess. Debt, no savings, and a job that felt like a grind. I decided to rebuild everything, and tech was my new best friend. I'm not saying this is the only way, but it worked for me.
## The Goal: A Digital Life
My goal was to create a digital life that was both cost-effective and flexible. I wanted to work remotely, have a reliable server for my projects, and keep my finances in check. I started by looking at two main options: cloud hosting and self-hosting.
## Cloud Hosting: The Easy Way
### DigitalOcean vs. Hetzner
DigitalOcean and Hetzner are the two big players in cloud hosting. DigitalOcean is known for its user-friendly interface and good support, while Hetzner is cheaper and has a reputation for reliability. I went with Hetzner because I wanted to save money. A $5 VPS from Hetzner is a steal compared to DigitalOcean's $10.
### The Trade-Off
The trade-off is that Hetzner's interface is less polished. You'll spend more time configuring things, but the cost savings can be significant. I haven't tested this on ARM, so your mileage may vary.
## Self-Hosting: The DIY Way
### Docker vs. Podman
For self-hosting, I looked at Docker and Podman. Docker is the more popular choice, but Podman is gaining traction. Podman is simpler and doesn't require a daemon, which means it's more secure. I love this tool but it has one fatal flaw: it's not as widely supported as Docker.
### The Setup
Setting up a server from scratch is a lot of work. I spent a week configuring my server, setting up Docker, and getting everything running. The community is genuinely split on this, but I found that Docker was more flexible for my needs.
## The Financial Side
### Budgeting Tools
I switched to a budgeting tool called YNAB (You Need A Budget). It's not free, but it's worth every penny. YNAB helped me get my finances in order and start saving. I set up a monthly budget and stuck to it. I haven't tried any other budgeting tools, but YNAB is the gold standard.
### Investing
I started investing in index funds through Vanguard. I set up automatic transfers and stuck to a dollar-cost averaging strategy. This is overkill for most people, but it worked for me. I've seen a 10% return on my investments over the past year.
## The Gotchas
### Security
Security is a big concern. I set up two-factor authentication (2FA) for everything and use a password manager. I also set up a firewall and updated my server regularly. This is a must for anyone self-hosting.
### Reliability
Reliability is another issue. I had a few outages with my Hetzner VPS, but I managed to fix them quickly. I haven't had any issues with DigitalOcean, but your mileage may vary.
## FAQ
### Q: Can I do this without tech knowledge?
A: Yes, but it will be harder. You can use managed services or hire someone to help you.
### Q: Is this approach scalable?
A: Yes, but it requires ongoing maintenance. You'll need to keep your server updated and monitor your finances regularly.
### Q: What if I need more resources?
A: You can upgrade your server or switch to a cloud provider. Hetzner and DigitalOcean both offer scalable solutions.
This is a real journey, and there are no easy answers. Tech can help you rebuild your life, but it requires effort and commitment.
