## The Problem: No Money, No Love
You spent 7-8 months building something for an audience that had no money. 10k visitors, 900 signups, 0 paying. This is a common tale in r/sideproject. The problem? Your audience is broke. 
## The Solution: Find a Monetizable Audience
First, find a monetizable audience. If your project is tech, consider targeting businesses or professionals who can afford to pay. If it's creative, look for people who can afford to buy your work. 
## Hosting: DigitalOcean vs. Hetzner
### DigitalOcean: The Easy Way
DigitalOcean is easy to set up and has a free tier. It's great for small projects. But it's not cheap. A $5/month droplet is $60/year. For 10k visitors, that's $600/year. 
### Hetzner: The Budget-Friendly Option
Hetzner is cheaper. A similar setup costs around $2/month. For 10k visitors, that's $240/year. But it's not as user-friendly. You need to set up SSH, configure DNS, and manage backups. 
## CMS: Jekyll vs. Hugo
### Jekyll: The Classic Choice
Jekyll is free, easy to set up, and has a large community. But it's slow. Jekyll sites can take 1-2 seconds to load. For 10k visitors, that's a lot of wasted time. 
### Hugo: The Faster Option
Hugo is faster. Hugo sites can load in 0.5-1 second. For 10k visitors, that's a big win. Hugo is also free and has a growing community. 
## Monetization: Ads vs. Sponsors
### Ads: The Quick Fix
Ads can bring in some money. Google AdSense is free and easy to set up. But it's not much. For 10k visitors, you might make $50-100/month. 
### Sponsors: The Long-Term Play
Sponsors can bring in more money. Find companies that can afford to sponsor your project. For 10k visitors, you might make $500-1000/month. But it takes time to build relationships. 
## Community: Reddit vs. Discord
### Reddit: The Wider Reach
Reddit has a wider reach. You can post in subreddits and get more visibility. But it's harder to build a community. 
### Discord: The Closer Bond
Discord is better for building a community. You can have live chats, voice calls, and more. But it takes time to build trust. 
## Final Thoughts
If you're building for an audience that has no money, find a way to monetize. Use Hetzner for hosting, Hugo for your CMS, and sponsors for monetization. And build a community on Discord. 
## FAQ
### Q: Can I use both DigitalOcean and Hetzner?
A: Yes, but it's more complex. You can use DigitalOcean for your CMS and Hetzner for your hosting. But it's not recommended. Stick to one provider for simplicity.
### Q: Should I use Google AdSense or a different ad network?
A: Google AdSense is free and easy, but it's not the best. Consider using a different ad network like Amazon Associates or ShareASale. They can bring in more money.
### Q: How can I build a community on Reddit?
A: Post in relevant subreddits, engage with the community, and build relationships. It takes time, but it's worth it.
