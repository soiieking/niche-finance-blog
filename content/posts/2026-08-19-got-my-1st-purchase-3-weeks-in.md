#### Where Things Got Interesting
The purchase that made the top of my list was **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (4GB)**. I have experience with hosting static webpages, but never with running and maintaining a side hustle. In my opinion, **buying this board has been the best decision ever.** I'm finally making progress on my project!
My goal is to run a static website without paying third-party hosting services. I considered the **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B** because I've heard that **Raspbian OS** supports hosting a web server without paying for the hardware. This is exactly what I need!
**NOTE:** 😅 I know this might sound overkill. For most people, hosting their work on a Raspberry Pi might be **unnecessary, except for highly specialized projects**. **Always weigh the pros and cons before choosing hosting solutions, especially when relying solely on them for mission-critical projects.**
However, **I'm preserving this article in the event it turns out to be well-written someday.**
I do expect some **compromises** with this kind of setup, such as slower load times or intermittent connectivity due to power supply. But, **I find it important to test the waters before committing to any tools and systems**. I would advise anyone having the same aspirations to **test this approach as well.**
**Slimy Hint:** If you're interested in alternatives to hosting static websites, there are a few other options you might want to consider!
#### Why I Won't Need More than Two of Them
**Moderator wrote**: _That really depends on what your needs for your server are. Bought more just for experimentation is a waste of money because you're only going to end up testing and not getting anything done._
Haha, good advice! I appreciated this response because it reminded me **not to overpromise, overspend or over-extend** myself. I'll admit **I over-invested in Raspberry Pi for hosting, but I'm learning my lesson.**

I'm starting off small. Two Pi's — **Winter Pi** — already got me to know enough **40 GB HD space to store 100,000 medium-sized files.** (So far, my website needs around 15GB for 10,000+ medium-sized files.)
**No Pi**: 0 MB
**2 WinterPis**: 40 GB
I figured I'd need **8GB of RAM, but I'm feeling pretty okay with 4GB**. If my website ends up needing more than that (which it probably will), I can always add more memory cards for an extra 1GB here or there if needed. **(I really hope so; being cheap is scary; life might get drastically different at some point.)**
**Current Usage**: 2GB RAM and MySQL for most of the other processes; might need more. Currently, the Pi takes about 15% of full CPU usage. **And note**: The process after that takes 15% and alternates with other processes. I hope I don't regret the philosophy after it's rendered. But then, the big upgrade project, which will deprive me of a good chance, is always great, and I think I just love it!
**NB**: **Not an expert** — if you're interested in low-speed projects and you think this might be what you need for your project as well, my rave review might be just the thing for you to help you decide!
