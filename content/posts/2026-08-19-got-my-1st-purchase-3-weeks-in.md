for a side project called **ServerLabs**. Check out my [GitHub repository](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/ServerLabs) if you're interested without breaking anything.

#### Where Things Got Interesting

The purchase that made the top of my list was **Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (4GB)**. I have experience with hosting static webpages, but never with running and maintaining a side hustle. In my opinion, **buying this board has been the best decision ever.** I'm finally making progress on my project!

My goal is to run a static website without paying third-party hosting services. I considered the **Raspberry Pi 4 ModeloB** because I've heard that **Raspbian OS** supports hosting a web server without paying for the hardware. This is exactly what I need!

**NOTE:** 😅 I know this might sound overkill. For most people, hosting their work on a Raspberry Pi might be **unnecessary, except for highly specialized projects**. **Always weigh the pros and cons before choosing hosting solutions, especially when relying solely on them for mission-critical projects.**

However, **I line by preserving this article in the event it turns out to be well-written someday.**

I do expect some **compromises** with this kind of setup, such as slower load times or intermittent connectivity due to power supply. But, **I find it important to test the waters before committing to any tools or systems**. I would advise anyone having the same aspirations to **test this approach as well.**

**Slimy Hint:** If you're interested in alternatives to hosting static websites, there are a few other options you might want to consider!

I'm starting off small. Two pins'**Winter Pi** with already got me to know enough **40 GB HD space to store 100,000 medium-sized files.** (So far, my website needs around 15GB for 10,000+ Medium sized files.)
**No Pi**: 0mb
**2 WinterPins**: 40GB

I figured I'd need **8GB of RAM, but I'm feeling pretty okay with 4GB**. If my website ends up needing more than that (which it probably will), I can always add more memory cards for an extra 1GB here or there if needed. **(I really hope so, being cheap is scary; life might get drastically different at some point.)**

**Current Usage**: 2GB RAM and MySQL the most of the other processes; might need more. Currently, the Pi takes about 15% of full CPU usage. **And note**: The process after that takes 15% and Alternating other processes. I hope I don’t regret the philosophy after it’s rendered.
