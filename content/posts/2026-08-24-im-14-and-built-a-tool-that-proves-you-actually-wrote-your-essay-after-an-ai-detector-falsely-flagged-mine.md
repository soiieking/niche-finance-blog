# AI Essay Detector: A Tool for the Detective's Daughter
## The Truth Hurts
Two weeks ago, I was cooking up a storm in my kitchen when I stumbled upon a concerning detail. My freshly brewed coffee, a dark and rich blend, was beautifully crafted but somehow bogus. I couldn't understand why—had I forgotten the secret ingredient? Upon closer inspection, I realized that the 'AI detector' I had used to verify the authenticity of my coffee's recipe was just a glorified AI-powered flagger. The renowned AI detectors, designed to catch cheating in school essays, had flagged my carefully made coffee as a potential scam. 
I was furious. Not only had my AI detector failed to identify the cheating in my coffee, but it had also failed to identify the cheating...in my coffee. It was a painful realization, but it sparked an idea.

This tool allows users to submit text, and it compares the data to a database of known cheating tendencies.

Each time my essay was deemed counterfeit by AI, my detector was sure to arrest the culprit. This time, it accurately identified the adulterated coffee in my hand—it wasn't just a case of a mislabelled container!
