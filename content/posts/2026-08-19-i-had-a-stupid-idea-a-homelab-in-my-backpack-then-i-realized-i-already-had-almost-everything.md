Armed with a Raspberry Pi 4, a battery pack for emergency charging, and a portable hard drive for data storage, I set out to create a portable server. It was an ambitious project, but my ego and enthusiasm outmatched any practical considerations.
As I shared my concept on r/selfhosted, I encountered a mix of excitement and skepticism. rc StevenButler had a point when he mentioned, "I love this idea, but you're gonna need better power management." And oh, do I regret not heeding those words.
The first roadblock was power. My 3500mAh battery pack could barely keep my Pi alive for an hour, let alone run resource-intensive applications. I tried to compensate by testing it against my cloud server — Pi did win on cost, but lost on reliability and performance.
