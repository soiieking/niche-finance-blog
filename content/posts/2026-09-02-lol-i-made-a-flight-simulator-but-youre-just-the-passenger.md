## The Side Project No One Asked For

Someone on r/sideproject built a flight simulator where you’re just the passenger. Yes, you sit there, watch the clouds go by, and pray the autopilot doesn’t glitch. That’s it. No controls, no objectives—just you, the seatbelt sign, and the ambient white noise of jet engines.

Why? Because why not.

It’s incredibly low-stakes, which makes it kind of genius. You’re not rewiring autopilots or coding neural networks that figure out taxi routes. This is purely vibes. And honestly, the crowd loved it—r/sideproject threads about this got "hell yeah" energy from people who would never dare touch a joystick in Microsoft's Flight Simulator.

Let’s talk about why this project works, and why it’s not as silly as it sounds.

---

## Peak Anti-Simulation Vibes  

So, flight sims typically cater to control freaks. I’m talking real-world pilots using $2000 PC setups simulating "emergency landing at JFK with 40 knots crosswind while the AI co-pilot screams." This project? The opposite. It skips all the stressful realism and says, "Hey, how about just *being on the plane*?"

The Redditor behind this said it started as a UI experiment in Unity. They wanted to mess around and accidentally stumbled into "passenger sim." Add a window view, cloud animations, and a semi-convincing airplane rumble sound—boom, an absurdly simple game that’s oddly calming. 

It’s almost meditative. Like those train window YouTube videos where you just watch the world pass by. Except, you know, this one *might* crash (spoiler: it doesn’t, relax).

---

## What’s the Deal with “Playable” Idleness?

Building a game where you more or less don’t “do” anything taps into a surprisingly active genre. Think idle games like Cookie Clicker or resource brokers like Factorio—but strip away progression mechanics or dopamine hacking. Pure aesthetic. This is for people who genuinely want to space out. 

That said, it’s a lot harder to get right than you’d think. A boring sim is, well, *boring*. But dumb little details sell it: the muffled stewardess announcement, the weird wing reflections, or the soft 'ding' when the seatbelt sign turns on. That’s why this worked.

Would I buy this? Probably not for money, not when Stardew Valley exists. But as a free or PWYW download on Itch.io? Hell yeah, I’d try it. Sometimes, you just want to feel like a guy sitting on a plane but without paying $7 for Wi-Fi.

---

## Where Could It Go?

The project is pretty barebones as of now—it looks like a Unity MVP slapped together in a weekend (because it was). But the top comment suggestions were on fire:  

1. **Emergency Landings:** Imagine turbulence, oxygen masks, or sudden pilot announcements like “Don’t worry, folks, we’re just gonna turn around for… reasons.”  
2. **Passenger Drama:** That one toddler screaming two rows back. A Karen arguing with cabin crew. It’s the little horrors that make a flight truly authentic.  
3. **Destination Randomizer:** You’re sitting in Seat 27A of a flight to *somewhere.* Make it random every time and throw in different airline vibes. (Lufthansa = efficiency; Ryanair = [pure RNG chaos](https://xkcd.com/1056/)).  
   
Right now, though, there’s no big monetization angle, unless someone goes nuts fleshing it out. Frankly, that makes it better. Not every project has to scale into a billion-dollar SaaS.

---

## Why This Works Even Without “Features”

The side project lottery doesn’t always reward utility. Sometimes you don’t need an app that "revolutionizes productivity." You can just make something because it entertains you; *that* energy attracts others. This simulator embodies that. 

Will this blow up and get a Steam page? Almost certainly not. But it’s a nice palette cleanser in a tech space full of ambitious overthinkers. Plus, the world *desperately needs* more dumb, fun things that don’t demand too much of our brains.

So yeah. If you’re stuck in your tenth iteration of a SaaS "minimal CRM for freelancers," maybe toss that aside for a day. Build your weird little autopilot-powered airplane ride. Worse case? You make something dumb but relaxing. Best case? You’re this person's passenger, watching clouds roll by while Reddit upvotes stack.
